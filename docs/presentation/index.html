The Packaging Trap: Multi-Layer Plastics

Why standard municipal recyclers throw clean packaging straight into landfills.

The Material Reality
  • Bonded Metal & Plastic:
    Chips and snack packets laminate aluminum foil between plastic film. Separating those layers is chemically difficult and commercially unprofitable.
  • 100% Landfill Destination:
    Because municipal plants cannot process multi-layer packaging, almost all snack wrappers are burned or dumped into local landfills.
  • Clean Waste in Schools:
    Canteens and classrooms generate sacks of clean snack wrappers daily with zero realistic collection logistics.
The EcoLoop Approach
  • Skip Separation, Heat-Compress:
    Instead of separating the foil, we partner to heat-press whole wrappers into dense boards that replace plywood.
  • Unified Circular Model:
    Unifying scattered school green crafts and outsourced heavy materials into one clean system.
  • Digital Transparency:
    Building an online catalog so buyers can see exact specifications and request real samples.

Replicating This Workflow: OpenCode

An open-source terminal agent that gives you the same autonomous capabilities.

Why OpenCode for Students?
  • 100% Free & Open Source:
    Created by Anomaly (GitHub: anomalyco/opencode). Zero subscriptions or vendor lock-in.
  • Lives in Your Terminal:
    Reads your project files, edits code, and runs local commands just like specialized agent harnesses.
  • Dual Execution Modes:
    Plan Mode reviews architecture safely; Build Mode executes real code changes.
