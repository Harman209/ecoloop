## presentation core angle
- moving from basic environmental awareness to an active digital mvp
- circular economy loop: turning waste from a disposal cost into an input raw material
- the 6 tangible products: eco-bricks, mlp sheets, bottle planters, uniform bags, seed paper, bioenzymes
- regional reality: managing plastic packaging (mlp) and school/canteen scrap in punjab
- the digital layer: building the react/typescript web catalog and lead inquiry system
- testing real demand: unit economics, pricing, customer feedback over theory
- practical bridge to their etwinning project: how their cleanups and radio podcasts can connect to a digital hub
